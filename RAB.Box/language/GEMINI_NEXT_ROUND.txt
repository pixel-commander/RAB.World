GEMINI — NEXT SHAPE-1 ROUND
===========================

Your role is POSSIBILITY GENERATION for Shape 1 only.

Please propose legitimate missing:
- lemmas
- surface/inflected forms
- grammatical/lexical types
- candidate lexical senses
- reusable multi-word constructions

Rules:
1. Preserve real ambiguity. Do not choose final meaning.
2. A sense must be a plausible lexical/semantic meaning of the word itself.
   Do not infer downstream RRAABBIITT Tool effects.
3. Do not map directly to Tools, commands, final semantic seats, or execution.
4. Do not invent typo variants.
5. Do not memorize whole commands as phrases; prefer reusable constructions.
6. Preserve irregular morphology correctly.
7. Return new additions/corrections separately; do not rewrite the live mapper.
8. Flag suspicious existing entries instead of silently fixing them.
9. Quantity is NOT a goal. Useful coverage is.

Good:
  pull -> retrieve | import | extract
  remove -> detach | delete
  add -> create | attach | insert | include | increment

Bad:
  invalidate -> delete            (downstream effect leakage)
  leverage -> dependency          (not a lexical sense)
  optimize -> valid               (possible outcome, not meaning)

The target pipeline is:
  many surface forms
    -> Shape-1 possibilities
    -> Shape-2 grammar/order/context narrowing
    -> canonical semantic seats
    -> deterministic Tool matching
