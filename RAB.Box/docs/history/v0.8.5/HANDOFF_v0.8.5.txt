GENERIC CHAIN LAW
-----------------
Everything invokable goes through the same runner contract. Stamp, normal Tool, finder/search Tool, counter, resolver, importer, and future provider wrappers are not special cases for chaining.

CURRENT
  -> CHILD TOOL
  -> typed/named returned seats
  -> runner seat bag
  -> CURRENT resumes
  -> later sibling/Step may reuse the same named seat

Explicit options win over inherited seats. A child never owns continuation; the runner owns parentTaskId, trace, seat packing, and resume.

LANGUAGE MEMORY RULE
--------------------
Do not store negative vocabulary just to remember that a word is unknown.
Unknown surface text can remain ephemeral in the active Step/check when grammar proves a
meaningful seat. It becomes durable only through a positive operator teaching receipt.
